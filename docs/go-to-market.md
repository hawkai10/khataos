# Go-to-Market & Onboarding

## Pricing

| Plan | Price | Included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free trial | ₹0 / 14 days | Full feature access |
| Standard | ₹25,000 / month | Up to 500 invoices/mo, 5 bank accounts |
| White-glove onboarding | ₹50,000 one-time | KhataOS team installs Tally connector, maps ledgers, configures approval rules |

The ₹50k white-glove tier is deliberately both a revenue line and a product
feedback channel for the first 50 customers.

## Self-serve onboarding journey (target ≤ 15 min to first cash)

```text
Sign up → Verify GSTIN → Connect bank via AA consent (OTP) → Cash visible
  → Tally connector installer (guided, health check)
  → Email forwarding rule (one click instructions)
  → Vendor master import from Tally
```

The product tracks onboarding step completion in-app; the Onboarding page in
this MVP implements the same flow with the simulator.

## Target segments (first 100 customers)

1. Manufacturing (₹100–300 crore) — heavy vendor payments, TDS, Tally-native.
2. Distribution/trading — high invoice volume, UPI/NEFT mix.
3. Professional services — 194J TDS, milestone invoicing, small team finance.

## Channel

- Direct sales led by founder/CFO-peers; CA firm partnerships as referral
